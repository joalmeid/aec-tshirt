# Alimenta Esta Corrida 2026

This directory supports the preparation of **Alimenta Esta Corrida 2026**, the flagship
charity event of **Associação Hélio Fumo – Inclusão Social Pelo Desporto**.

## Event details

- Event: "Alimenta Esta Corrida" — 5th edition
- Date: Saturday, 14 November 2026, 08:30–18:30 (10 consecutive hours)
- Location: Praça do Campo Pequeno, Lisbon
- Format: solidarity run/walk, free entry — participants "pay" with a food donation instead
  of a race fee. Founder Hélio Fumo runs continuously for the full 10 hours; participants
  join for whatever distance they choose, alongside invited public figures.
- 2026 targets: 1,400 participants, 6.5 tons of food collected
- Beneficiary institutions (distribute donated goods to families in need): Paróquia da
  Póvoa de Santo Adrião, Junta de Freguesia das Avenidas Novas

## Corporate sponsorship

- `Carta de pedido Apoio às Empresas 2026 VF.docx` is a **template** sponsorship-request
  letter — currently drafted addressed to Microsoft Portugal. When preparing outreach to a
  new company, personalize the addressee, reference, and date, keeping the rest of the
  pitch structure.
- Ask types offered to sponsors: financial sponsorship/donations, food donations, donation
  of goods/equipment/services, employee engagement in food collection, internal/external
  promotion of the event.
- Benefits offered in return: official "Mecenas" or "Parceiro Técnico" status, brand
  visibility at high-traffic points of the event, presence in media/social coverage,
  employee participation (employer branding angle), association with a high-impact social
  project.
- Past partners/sponsors to reference (2025 edition): Junta de Freguesia das Avenidas
  Novas, Compressport, Scarpa, Mimosa, Izidoro, Hotel Roma, Mais Nutrição, Magtronics,
  Farmácia Miranda, Pastelaria O Capote, Pão da Vila, Talho da Calçada da Estrela,
  Plastrofa, Antipest, Fotos do Pio, BA&N.
- Media reach so far: 100+ news stories across national media, 500K+ people reached on
  social media.

## Working conventions

- Source material and public-facing communication for this event are in Portuguese
  (PT-PT). Keep sponsor letters, social posts, and press materials in PT unless a request
  specifically targets an international audience or company.
- When drafting new sponsor letters, follow the existing letter's ask/offer structure
  rather than rewriting it from scratch.
